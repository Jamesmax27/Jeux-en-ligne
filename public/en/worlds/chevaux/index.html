Horse and pony games

The world of horses holds a special place for many kids, and it has its own page. The games take place at the stable, in the paddock or at the riding school: depending on the game, you get a pony ready and brush it, choose its outfit, feed it, gallop, clear jumps or answer riding quiz questions. Riding vocabulary slips in naturally (coat, mane, halter, trot, gallop), along with grooming routines and respect for the animal. Young riders and dreamers who have never been near a pony each have something to enjoy: the former will be proud to explain the words they recognize, the latter may discover a wish to try a first pony ride.
