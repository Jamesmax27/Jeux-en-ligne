The Spellbook of Seven Seals

Play the cards in your spellbook to free the bewitched creatures, build up your deck and break the seven seals in 3 acts!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🚲Ages 7-8♟️Strategy🃏Board and card games🎭Role-playing and progression🧚Fantasy and magic

About the game

A young wizard opens a spellbook to help bewitched creatures. Here, the creatures are not defeated: each one is freed and gets its smile back. The adventure crosses three acts, the sleeping forest, the ember mountains and the storm citadel, all the way to the heart of the spellbook. Along the way you meet the Blue jelly, the Queen bee, the Ember dragonling and the Frost owl, before facing the Keeper of the Seals. Your deck starts with ten cards and grows as you win: the game has 60 attack, defense, spell and power cards. Between battles, you pick your path: campfire, the owl's shop, treasure, surprise or elite. Relics give you lasting effects, and each broken seal brings magic back to the forest.

How to play

Your goal: free all the creatures before your health runs out. Each turn, you get 3 energy points and 5 cards. Tap a card (or click it), then tap it again or tap the enemy you are aiming at. Out of energy? Tap “End turn”. On a keyboard, 1 to 9 picks a card, the arrows switch cards or targets, Enter or Space plays it and F ends the turn. Each level gives up to 3 stars: winning, few turns, little health lost. After a defeat, you can try again for one fewer star. Each of the 12 levels adds something new, such as burn, freeze or thorns.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 8.

Skills it builds: anticipating enemy moves, managing energy, building a deck of cards, strategic choices
