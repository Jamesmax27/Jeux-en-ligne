Sleigh Gift Packing

Gift night is coming: fit presents of every shape into the sleigh basket without leaving a single gap!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🪁Ages 5-6🧩Puzzles and logic🎄Seasons and holidays

About the game

Gift night is almost here and the sleigh basket is still empty! Drag each present with your finger and tap it to turn it: you have to fill every square without leaving a single gap. Across 24 levels and 4 villages, you'll find logs that take up space, fragile presents that must go right at the top, big gifts that fill 5 squares, frozen presents that can't turn, presents that go in the corner matching their ribbon color, baskets with special shapes and even a magic basket that grows. Pack carefully before the moon sets, and hurry when dawn is coming. A perfect basket starts the starry delivery: you drop presents down the chimneys to win bells and decorate your sleigh. Each village you finish also gives you a cuddly reindeer.

How to play

The goal: fill every square of the basket with the presents from the workbench. Drag a present into the basket with your finger or the mouse, and tap it on the workbench to give it a quarter turn. On the keyboard: left and right arrows to choose, Space to turn, Enter to pick up and then place, arrows to move, Z to take back the last present, H for a hint. Each present you place makes the moon go down: place them all right the first time to win 3 stars (2 at most with a hint). Too many moves, and the moon sets: you have to try again. Over the 24 levels, the elves' conveyor belt and races against dawn show up too.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 6.

Skills it builds: spatial awareness, turning shapes in your head, logic, planning ahead
