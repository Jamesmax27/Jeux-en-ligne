Plume Takes Flight

Flap your wings to guide Plume, the little bird, home to his nest across 50 skies of rain, snow, storms and stars!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🧸Ages 3-4🕹️Arcade🎯Aiming and shooting🌳Nature and gardens🦁Wild animals

About the game

Plume, a little orange bird, wants to fly home to his nest. To get there, he crosses 50 skies spread over 10 chapters, from the meadow all the way to space, passing the coast, the mountains, the city, the night forest and a great storm. Each kind of weather has its own effect: in the rain he gets heavier; in the snow he floats and slides; at night he only sees what is around him, and fireflies make his light bigger; in space he floats almost weightlessly. Along the way he meets hot-air balloons, planes, seagulls, eagles, drones, bats, rockets and UFOs. Before each level, an animated screen shows the weather and what is new. At the end of the journey, Plume faces the UFO mothership.

How to play

The goal: bring Plume to his nest; each bump costs him one of his 3 hearts. Tap the screen, click or press Space, the up arrow or W to flap your wings. From level 6, hold down to glide. From level 12, seeds fill up the Dash (button, D or Shift): Plume zooms straight through everything. Collect snowflakes in the snow, or stardust in space from level 42, then press Throw (F or X) to send snowballs or stars. P or Escape pauses the game. With no bumps at all you earn 3 stars; with just one, 2; otherwise, 1. The 50 levels get faster and faster, then the Endless sky opens up.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 4.

Skills it builds: reflexes, hand-eye coordination, anticipating obstacles, adapting to changing weather
