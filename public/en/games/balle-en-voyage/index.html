Ball Journey

Roll your 3D ball from platform to platform up to the star, with checkpoint flags along the way!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🪁Ages 5-6🦘Platformer🔷Abstract

About the game

Ball Journey is a 3D platform game. You guide a red ball with white spots along a path of colorful blocks floating in a sky full of clouds. At the end of the path, a big golden star hovers above a green block: that is the finish. Along the way, blue gems bob above some blocks and flags mark the checkpoints. The three levels keep getting longer: the gaps get wider, the blocks get narrower and, from the second level on, some of them slide from side to side. The blocks are not all at the same height, so every jump needs good aim. The levels are always the same: you can learn the route and come back with fewer falls and more gems.

How to play

Your goal: bring the ball to the star. With your finger or the mouse, the four arrows at the bottom left steer the ball and the big JUMP button makes it jump. On the keyboard, use the arrow keys (or W, A, S, D and Z, Q, S, D) and the Space bar to jump. Collect the blue gems by touching them with the ball. One block in five has a flag that turns green when you land on it: if you fall, you start again from there. No lives to lose: the game only counts your gems and your falls. Level 1: 12 fixed blocks. Level 2: 18 blocks, a few of them moving. Level 3: 24 narrower blocks, a few of them moving too.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 6.

Skills it builds: dexterity, hand-eye coordination, spatial awareness, precise jumping
