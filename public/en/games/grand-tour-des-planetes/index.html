The Grand Planet Tour

Put the planets in order around the Sun, spot the odd ones out and guess who is talking in the riddles!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🚲Ages 7-8🧩Puzzles and logic❓Quizzes and trivia🚀Space

About the game

The Sun is waiting for its eight planets! In this puzzle and quiz game, you put Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune back in their places. At first, shadows show the shape of each planet. Then only a short sentence helps you remember the order: “My Very Easy Method Just Speeds Up Naming”. Next, the Moon, Pluto, a comet or an asteroid sneak in among the planets, and some planets hide behind a riddle. Every object you put in the right spot teaches you a secret, like the rust that makes Mars red, and fills one of the 12 cards in your space album. In the last level, everything is mixed together and a comet races against you.

How to play

The goal: put each object in space in the right spot. Tap a planet at the bottom of the screen, then its orbit (spot 1 is the closest to the Sun), or drag it with your finger or the mouse. On the keyboard, the arrow keys choose a planet, keys 1 to 9 put it on a spot and the I key sends it to the odd-one-out box. Zero mistakes give you 3 stars, one or two mistakes give you 2. You never lose: after a mistake, a hint helps you. One by one, the 12 levels add the “Who am I?” quiz, odd ones out, sorting by size, riddles and a comet to beat for extra points.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 7.

Skills it builds: order and size of the planets, reading riddles, memory
