Letter Balloons

A letter is shown: quickly pop all the balloons with that letter before they fly away!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🧸Ages 3-4🔤Words and letters⚡Reflexes and quick taps🔷Abstract

About the game

In a blue sky with a few clouds, balloons in six colors rise from the bottom of the screen, gently swaying. Each one carries a big capital letter, from A to Z. At the top, a white box shows the letter to find. Your job is to spot the right balloons among all the others and pop them before they vanish at the top! Each right balloon fades away with a little sound and a “+1” that floats up. The faster you go, the more letters you see and the faster the balloons rise. A round lasts just one minute: start again right away to do better and earn the title “Letter champion!”.

How to play

The goal: pop as many balloons as you can that carry the letter shown at the top, before the 60-second timer runs out. Tap a balloon with that letter, or click it with the mouse, to pop it. On the keyboard, type the letter: the highest balloon with that letter pops. Each right balloon gives you one point. A wrong balloon shakes, but you lose nothing. There are no lives and no numbered levels: just one round of one minute. Every five right balloons, the screen clears, a new letter arrives, and the balloons rise faster and more often. At the end, you get “Well done!” with 10 balloons or more and “Letter champion!” with 25 or more.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 4.

Skills it builds: recognizing capital letters, visual attention, reaction speed
