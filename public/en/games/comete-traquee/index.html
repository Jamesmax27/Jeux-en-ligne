Comète on the Run

As Comète, a masked hero, save the city at night without getting caught on camera by the trackers!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🪁Ages 5-6🕹️Arcade♟️Strategy🏙️City and jobs

About the game

Night falls on the city, and everyone follows Comète, a masked hero, on the “Where is Comète?” app. With every sighting, the trackers, and later the press drones, head for his last known position to snap a clear photo. You guide Comète on a top-down map of the district, between streets, tree-filled parks and rooftops. Incidents pop up one after another: a cat stuck on a shop sign, a stuck elevator, a lost child, a dog fallen into the canal. The sightings feed scrolls live with messages from the locals. From the Old Port to Low Island, every district has a brand-new map. The fun is all in getting there fast without being seen.

How to play

Rush to each incident before the countdown ends. With your finger, use the D-pad, swipe or tap the map. On a keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space to pause. In the street, you get spotted fast; on the rooftops, almost never (except by a drone), but every step on a roof wears out the grapple, which recharges on the ground. A tracker or a drone on your square, or a missed incident, costs one of your three masks; each district you secure gives one back, and with no masks left, the game ends. A quick rescue earns more points. There's no last level: 3 to 6 rescues per district, 2 to 7 trackers, up to three drones and less time; after eight districts, the same ones come back tougher.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 6.

Skills it builds: anticipation, map reading, quick decision-making, risk management
