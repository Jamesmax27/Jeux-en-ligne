The Cloud Bakery

In your bakery up on a cloud, mix, bake and decorate the cakes the birds draw in their speech bubbles!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🪁Ages 5-6🩹Simulation and care🏪Management and tycoon🧁Cooking and food

About the game

Welcome to the Cloud Bakery! Sweet-toothed birds fly in to order a cake, drawn in a bubble above their heads. Drag the right ingredients into the bowl, stir by circling your finger, take the cake out of the oven at just the right moment, then pick the icing and place the strawberries, flowers or candles exactly like the picture. Over 12 days and 4 seasons, you'll stack layer cakes, serve several customers at once and even bake an order for the royal swan. Earn coins and stars to discover new recipes, buy star-, flower- and cloud-shaped cake tins and decorate your shop window!

How to play

Your goal: give each bird the cake from its bubble before its patience runs out. With your finger or the mouse, drag the ingredients into the bowl, circle around the bowl to stir, take the cake out when the needle is in the green zone, tap the right icing and place the decorations. On a keyboard, the arrow keys choose an item or move the cursor on the cake, Space or Enter confirms, the number keys 1 to 9 pick an item, and Esc or P pauses. Customers 50% happy on average: 1 star; 70%: 2; 85%: 3; below that, you have to play the level again. The 24 levels add cake tins, layers, customers in a hurry, several birds at once and a hidden picture.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 5.

Skills it builds: observing a model, precise finger movements, visual memory, planning
