The Squirrels' Grocery Store

Run the till at the forest grocery store: add up the animals' shopping and give change in acorns!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🚲Ages 7-8🔢Maths and numbers🏪Management and tycoon🏙️City and jobs🦁Wild animals

About the game

Welcome behind the counter of the squirrels' grocery store! Rabbits, owls and foxes come to buy hazelnuts, mushrooms and jam: tap each item to ring it up, type the total, then give change with coins worth 1, 2, 5 and 10 acorns. Over 12 days you'll meet "buy 2, get 1 free" deals, customers in a hurry, discount coupons, change to give with as few coins as possible, deliveries to fill the shelves and orders to pack. Win up to 3 stars per level, unlock new aisles and pretty shop signs, and collect the 12 rare customers who travel from far away. A gentle maths game to practise adding and subtracting while having fun.

How to play

Your goal: serve every customer of the day without losing all your hearts. Tap each item to ring it up, type the total on the keypad and confirm, then tap coins in the drawer to give change. On a keyboard, Enter rings up an item, number keys type the total, keys 1, 2, 5 and 0 give a coin worth 1, 2, 5 or 10 acorns, Enter confirms and Escape pauses. You have 5 hearts and each mistake costs one. A level you win earns 1 star, 2 with two mistakes or fewer, 3 with no mistakes and a quick time. The 24 levels, morning and afternoon over 12 days, add deals, discount coupons, customers in a hurry, deliveries and orders.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 7.

Skills it builds: addition, subtraction, giving change, mental maths
