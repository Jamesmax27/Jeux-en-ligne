The Tractor Rally

Race your tractor through meadows, mud and rain: overtake the 3 rivals, collect hay and finish first!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🪁Ages 5-6🏁Racing🚗Vehicles🐄Farm

About the game

The Tractor Rally is a top-down race along the farm paths. Your tractor drives forward on its own: you steer it to overtake three rivals and cross the finish line first. Each track adds a surprise: mud puddles that slow you down, a rain shower that makes you slide, hay bales that give you a turbo boost, a shortcut hidden in the wheat, cows crossing the path, morning fog, and then a hay trailer that can tip over. The paths get narrower and twistier all the way to the grand finale. Your stars unlock new tractor colors and funny horns in the garage, like Moo or Cock-a-doodle-doo, and then a bonus track in the vineyards at sunset.

How to play

The goal: finish first, ahead of the three other tractors. Slide your finger left or right to turn, or drag with the mouse button held down. On the keyboard, the ← and → arrows (or A and D) steer, Space honks the horn and P pauses. The horn makes animals on the path hurry up. On the first three tracks, 1st place earns 3 stars, 2nd earns two and 3rd earns one. After that, 1st place earns two, 2nd or 3rd earns one, and collecting enough hay adds one more. In 4th place you get no star, and you need one to unlock the next track. There are 12 tracks, plus a bonus track that opens at 18 stars.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 5.

Skills it builds: reflexes, anticipating obstacles, hand-eye coordination, precise movements
