Touch Grooming – Groom your pony with a fingertip

A muddy pony is waiting in its stall. Rub its coat, pick its hooves and comb out its mane with real finger moves. When it's 100% clean, it wins a ribbon!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🧸Ages 3-4🩹Simulation and care🐴Horses

About the game

In Touch Grooming, a muddy pony is waiting for you in its stall, between the wooden wall and the straw. It gets a random name, like Caramel, Noisette or Tornado, and one of seven coats: chestnut, bay, palomino, gray, black, dun or roan. Your job is to clean it with real grooming tools: the curry comb, the hoof pick and the mane comb. The pony reacts to every move: its ears turn, its tail swishes, it closes its eyes with pleasure and talks in a speech bubble (“That tickles!”, “There was a pebble”). Scratch it at the withers, its favorite spot, and watch its lip wiggle. When it finally shines, a colored ribbon appears in its mane and tail.

How to play

Your goal: fill the cleanliness gauge to 100%. Pick a tool at the bottom of the screen, then slide your finger over the pony, or drag with the mouse while holding the button down; no keyboard needed. With the curry comb, rub off the mud spots. With the hoof pick, tap a hoof circled in pink: it appears up close, so scrape from left to right to knock out the pebbles. With the mane comb, swipe down over the 4 knots in the mane. Cuddle doesn’t clean, it just makes the pony happy. No levels, stars, timer or losing: at the end, “Another pony” brings in a new, muddy one.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 4.

Skills it builds: fine motor skills, precise hand movements, caring for horses, grooming vocabulary
