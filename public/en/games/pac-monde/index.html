Maze Muncher

Eat every dot in a region to open its door: a new maze appears and the world keeps growing, up to 10 regions!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🪁Ages 5-6🕹️Arcade🌀Mazes🔷Abstract

About the game

A little yellow muncher opens its mouth wide as it rolls through a dark maze with glowing walls. At first, the world has only one region. Once every dot in it has been eaten, a sealed door with golden bars opens and a new region appears right next to it, in its own color. The old regions stay open: you can go back through them, and their ghosts still roam there, because ghosts never leave their own region. The corridors are drawn at random in each game, so the world never looks like the last one. A mini-map shows the open regions and the next door, and an arrow points the way when you wander off. Region after region, the ghosts become more numerous and faster.

How to play

The goal: eat every dot in the current region without letting a ghost touch you. Swipe on the screen or tap the arrow pad; on a keyboard, use the arrow keys, W A S D or Z Q S D, and P to pause. A big dot turns the ghosts blue for 7 seconds: catch them for 200, 400, 800, then 1,600 points. Cherries are worth 200 points, each opened door 500. You have 3 lives and no stars: only your score counts. There are 10 regions: the first has 2 ghosts, the last ones have 6, and they speed up a little from one region to the next.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 6.

Skills it builds: reflexes, reading a map, planning moves ahead, quick decisions
