Pony Apples

Slide the pony to catch the apples and carrots falling from the sky!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🧸Ages 3-4🕹️Arcade⚡Reflexes and quick taps🐴Horses

About the game

In Pony Apples, a reddish-brown pony with a dark forelock is very hungry. Red apples and, now and then, orange carrots fall from the sky toward the meadow, under the sun and the drifting clouds. Your job: move the pony left and right so it catches them in its mouth. Each time it does, it gives a little hop, opens its mouth wide and bursts of color fly out. The game lasts as long as you have lives left: the higher your score, the faster and more often the food falls. So you have to choose where to go: a carrot is worth more, but every apple that lands in the grass costs you one of your three lives.

How to play

The goal: catch as many apples and carrots as you can before you lose your three lives. On a touchscreen, tap or slide your finger left or right: the pony follows. With a mouse, just move the pointer. On a keyboard, use the left and right arrow keys, and press Enter or Space to start or restart. An apple is worth 1 point, a carrot 3 points. A missed carrot costs nothing, but an apple that falls in the grass dims one of the three apples at the top of the screen. There are no levels or stars: the speed and the number of falling items go up with your score. After the third missed apple, your score appears.

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 4.

Skills it builds: hand-eye coordination, reflexes, attention, quick choices between apples and carrots
