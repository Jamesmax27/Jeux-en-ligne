Dinosaur Egg Island

Guide a little dino across a prehistoric island: push logs, fix vine bridges and bring the lost eggs back to their nests!

Play

Free, nothing to install. Open it once with the internet, then play it again offline.

🚲Ages 7-8🧩Puzzles and logic🗺️Adventure and exploration🦕Dinosaurs and prehistory

About the game

On a prehistoric island, green, blue and pink eggs have gone missing. You guide a little green dino wearing an explorer's hat, from the beach to the summit of the volcano. Every zone is a puzzle: a log pushed into the river becomes a bridge, a vine fixes a broken bridge, and a triceratops friend pushes the giant ferns aside. In the caves it is dark, so you follow glowing footprints; near the volcano, geysers spit steam. Each time you complete a zone, an egg hatches in your nest: a baby compsognathus, a baby diplodocus, a baby pteranodon and seven more. Some of them give you a power, like Sniff, which shows the way, Fly, which carries you over an obstacle, or Song, which puts the geysers to sleep.

How to play

Bring each egg back to the nest of its color. Tap a square: the dino walks there by itself. Tap a log, a fern or a broken bridge: the dino walks up to it and acts. The powers and undo also have their own buttons on the screen. On a keyboard: arrow keys to move, Space or Enter for Sniff, V then an arrow key for Fly, C for Song, Z or U to undo a step, P or Esc to pause. The 3 fossils in each zone give you stars, from 1 to 3. There are no lives and no timer, but fewer steps earn more points. The 10 zones open one by one; from the 2nd to the 9th, each one adds something new: rivers, ravines, caves, geysers…

Who is it for?

Recommended from age 7.

Skills it builds: planning moves, logical thinking, spatial awareness, sorting by color
