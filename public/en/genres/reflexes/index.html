Reflex and speed games

Everything happens in a split second. Tap a target before it disappears, react as soon as a color changes, catch what's falling, or on the contrary hold your finger back when you mustn't touch: these games put your reaction speed to the test. Rounds are often short, which makes you want to play again right away to grab a few more points, and the pace usually speeds up from level to level. What improves: speed, coordination between what your eyes see and what your finger does, and the ability to stay focused despite all the action. Since there's little to read, the simplest ones work for kids around 4 or 5. These games are intense: after a few rounds, a break to catch your breath does you good.
