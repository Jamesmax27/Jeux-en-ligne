Online board and card games

Pieces moving across a board, dice to roll, cards to draw, flip or play at the right moment: board and card games bring back the fun of playing around a table. Classic rules or brand-new inventions, they almost always mix luck and thinking. You learn to follow a rule, wait your turn, count and guess what the other player will do. The simplest ones can be played from 5 or 6. No game is played against strangers on the internet: you play against the computer, or with several players on the same screen when the game allows it. Side by side, you also learn to lose with a smile.
