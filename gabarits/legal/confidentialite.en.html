This policy explains what data the Free Micro Games website processes, why, for how long, and how to exercise your rights, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés).

In brief

  • You are never asked for an account, a name or an email address to play, vote or send a request. Your player profile (level, badges, stars) is anonymous: a number and a nickname, “Joueur” (Player) followed by a number until you choose another one.
  • The website never stores your IP address as such: only a fingerprint computed with a secret key, which cannot be turned back into the address, is kept.
  • Advertisements are always non-personalised: they are never chosen based on your browsing.
  • Audience measurement (Google Analytics) only runs if you accept it in the cookie banner.
  • No data is sold or rented.

Data controller

The data controller is the website publisher, a private individual acting on a non-professional basis (see the Legal Notice). Contact: contact@freemicrogames.com.

Data processed, purposes and legal bases

Votes (thumbs up, thumbs down)

When you vote for the first time, your browser creates a random identifier, which is kept on your device. The server only receives a fingerprint of it (SHA-256). For each vote, it records: this fingerprint, the game, the vote (for or against), its date, the country you are connecting from (two-letter code) and a fingerprint of your IP address calculated with a secret key (for an IPv6 address, a fingerprint of its network part, shared by the devices of a household). The IP address itself is never stored.

Purposes: counting votes, keeping only one vote per game and per device, ranking the games, and detecting abuse. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in providing a reliable ranking (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Game session statistics

When you leave a game, the website records: the game, the length of the session (2 hours at most), the country you are connecting from, the type of device (phone or computer), the game's first technical error if there was one, and a fingerprint of your IP address calculated in the same way (for an IPv6 address, of its network part, which may be shared by a few neighbouring households). It also records what the game reports about the session: whether it was won, and the highest level (or score) reached; whether the same game had been started on the device less than 30 minutes earlier (replay); the time the game was started; and a random identifier generated by your browser (“fmg-visiteur”, unrelated to your votes and to Google Analytics, renewed after 13 months at most), which is used to group the parts of a single session. If you have refused cookies in the banner, this identifier changes each time a game is opened and nothing is kept on your device for these statistics.

Purposes: displaying the “Trending” section (the most played games of the week), counting different players so that a single device or a bot cannot skew this ranking; producing anonymous statistics for each game, viewed only by the publisher (number of starts, length, quick exits, replays, completed games), to find out which games children enjoy and improve future ones; and detecting games that do not work. These statistics are not shared with anyone and are never used for advertising. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); audience measurement limited to this internal use is exempt from consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, CNIL guidelines), and you can object to it by refusing cookies.

Player profile (level, badges, stars, leaderboard)

On the first visit, the website creates an anonymous player profile to award experience, levels, badges and stars, and to restore progress from one device to another. The server records: a profile number; a nickname (by default “Player” followed by a random number; you can choose another one, 3 to 16 characters, refused if it is rude or already taken: never write your real name, as it is shown on the leaderboard); the chosen avatar and frame; experience, level and badges; for each game, the playing time, the dates of the first and last session, whether the game was completed and the best number of stars; daily gains (for the weekly and monthly leaderboards); the days of your visits (your device's day) and your streak of consecutive days; for multiplayer games, the result of each round (won or not, number of real players, length, number of moves); and, if you ask for it, a 4-word save code. Your device receives a random secret token (the server only keeps a SHA-256 fingerprint of it). To limit abuse (profile and code creation, code attempts, nickname changes), the website keeps for a few days a fingerprint of the IP address calculated with a secret key, never the address itself. Playing time is measured by the website, never by the game; no name, email address or location data is requested.

The public leaderboard shows, for the top 100 players of the week or month, the nickname, avatar, level and points; any player can report an inappropriate nickname, which goes back to “Player” followed by a number after several reports. The save code is only a key that links another device to the same profile (it contains no data); entering a code is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile and the number of attempts is limited. Only give it to your parents: anyone who knows it can play with your progress.

Every day, the website also computes anonymous indicators, viewed only by the publisher (return rates after 1, 7 and 30 days, streak lengths, distribution of levels, completed games, save codes created and used, multiplayer rounds), to find out what makes players come back.

Purposes: providing the player profile (progress, rewards, leaderboard, saving across devices), protecting it against cheating and bots, and improving the website. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in providing and protecting this service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); what is kept on your device for this service is strictly necessary for the service you use (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act).

Requests sent with the form (suggest a game, report a problem)

The “Suggest a game or report a problem” form does not ask for a name or an email address. It sends the type of request, the game concerned, a title and a description, and it asks you not to write any personal information in them. The request is stored as a private record (“issue”) in the website's GitHub repository, which only the publisher can see. It is first read by an artificial intelligence (Claude, by Anthropic), which sets aside insulting messages, advertising and off-topic requests, then by the publisher. To limit submissions (3 per hour and 10 per day) and block abuse, the website keeps, with the date of each request, a fingerprint of your IP address calculated with a secret key (for an IPv6 address, of its network part); the address itself is never stored, and this fingerprint is not sent to GitHub or Anthropic.

Purposes: receiving game ideas and problem reports, sorting and handling them, and protecting the form against bots and abuse (3 abuses within 24 hours block submissions for 7 days; a repeat offence blocks them permanently). Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in improving and protecting the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Playing with others (multiplayer games)

In a multiplayer game, the button that starts a game with other players opens a connection to the site's game server (multi.freemicrogames.com, hosted by Cloudflare); nothing is sent until you tap it. The site only sends the game, a fingerprint of its rules and the moves played. There is no account and no messaging between players: other players only see a coloured seat and your moves. The server keeps the state of the game in progress (seats, moves, scores) and the number of players in each game; it does not record your IP address. If you have a player profile, the website also sends the game server a signed ticket (profile number, game, expiry time), obtained after a Cloudflare Turnstile check, so that the result of each round is added to your profile (see “Player profile”). Nothing is stored on your device for these games.

Purpose: letting several people play together, with robots in the free seats. Legal basis: the publisher's legitimate interest in providing this service you request (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Audience measurement (Google Analytics)

If you accept cookies in the banner, the website uses Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited); otherwise, it is not loaded and receives no data. Google Analytics records: pages viewed, game starts (name of the game), votes (name of the game; for, against or cancelled), language, type of device, browser, country and approximate city, derived from the IP address, which is not stored. A random identifier is kept in the _ga and _ga_HTQ7RNYW3J cookies (see the Cookie Policy). Google Signals and ad personalisation signals are disabled: this data is never used to show you advertisements.

Purposes: finding out how many people play, which games they enjoy, and improving the website. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act), which you can withdraw at any time using the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page; the website's Google Analytics cookies are then deleted.

Protection against bots (Cloudflare Turnstile)

Each vote, each request sent with the form, each save code entered and each entry into a multiplayer game with a profile is checked by Cloudflare Turnstile, which is only loaded when you send it. Cloudflare analyses technical data (IP address, browser characteristics) to tell a person from a bot. Legal basis: legitimate interest in protecting votes, the form and profiles against fraud (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Hosting

Like any hosting provider, Cloudflare processes connection data (IP address, pages requested, date and time) to display the website and protect it against attacks. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Google AdSense advertising

The website may display a Google AdSense advertisement, at the bottom of the home page only (never in the games). These ads are always non-personalised and requested with child-directed treatment: no targeting based on your interests, no remarketing. Google may set advertising cookies only if you accept them in the banner; otherwise, no advertising cookie is read or set. Google also processes technical data (IP address, browser) to display the ad, count it and prevent fraud.

Legal basis: your consent for cookies (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act), which you can withdraw at any time using the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

Data kept on your device

The language you choose, your votes, your progress in the games, a copy of your player profile (with its secret token, the playing time not yet sent and the date of your last visit), your cookie choice and the games kept for offline play are stored in your browser, not on our servers. Details are given in the Cookie Policy.

Emails

If you write to us, your email address and your message are used only to reply to you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Retention periods

  • Votes: for as long as they are used to rank the game. Tapping the same thumb a second time cancels the vote, which is then deleted from the database.
  • Game session statistics: each session is kept for 60 days, then automatically deleted; only anonymous totals per game and per day remain (number of starts, lengths, votes of the day), with no identifier or fingerprint.
  • Random statistics identifier (“fmg-visiteur”, on your device): 13 months at most, then a new one is generated.
  • Playing with others: the state of a game is deleted as soon as its last player leaves; a game with no news is forgotten after 4 hours.
  • Requests sent with the form: kept in the website's private history, without any IP address or fingerprint; you can ask for them to be deleted. IP address fingerprint linked to a request: 13 months at most; submission counter: 2 days; abuse records: 90 days; block: 7 days, or no time limit in the event of a permanent ban.
  • Player profile: for as long as you keep visiting the website; a profile with no visit for 2 years is deleted together with everything attached to it (games, gains, visit days, devices, round results). Daily gains, visit days and round results: 13 months. Anti-abuse records linked to the IP address fingerprint: 2 days (blocks on code attempts: 7 days at most after the last failure). Loyalty indicators: anonymous daily totals, with no identifier, kept without time limit.
  • Cookie choice: 6 months, after which the banner is shown to you again.
  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_HTQ7RNYW3J): 13 months at most, not extended on each visit.
  • Audience measurement data in Google Analytics: 2 months, then automatically deleted (only anonymous totals remain in the reports).
  • Google AdSense cookies: 13 months at most (see the Cookie Policy).
  • Connection and security data processed by Cloudflare: for a limited period, in accordance with Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Emails: for the time needed to handle your request, then 1 year at most.
  • Data kept on your device: until you delete it (browser settings).

Recipients and transfers outside the European Union

Only the publisher has access to the data recorded by the website. It is processed on the publisher's behalf by Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting, database, Turnstile), by GitHub, Inc. (private storage of the requests sent with the form), by Anthropic (reading of these requests by the Claude AI) and, for audience measurement and advertising, by Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC). These companies may process data in the United States; these transfers are governed by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission of 10 July 2023) and by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, deletion (erasure), objection and restriction of processing, as well as the right to data portability and the right to set instructions regarding what happens to your data after your death. You can withdraw your consent to cookies at any time, as easily as you gave it.

To exercise these rights, write to contact@freemicrogames.com. You will receive a reply within one month. As the website knows neither your name nor your email address, it is not always possible to find the data linked to your device: you can also cancel your votes yourself and delete the website's data at any time in your browser settings. To have your player profile deleted, send us its save code (“My profile” page).

Complaints to the CNIL

If you believe that your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France, website: www.cnil.fr.

Children and minors

Free Micro Games is intended for families, including children. The website offers no accounts and no chat between players, and does not ask for any personal information. The only free text that other players can see is the profile nickname, which is optional (“Player” followed by a number by default), filtered by the server (rude words, reserved names, no more than 4 digits in a row) and can be reported; the profile page reminds players never to write their real name in it. Its only form (suggest a game, report a problem) publishes nothing: requests are read only by the sorting AI and by the publisher, and the form reminds users not to write their name, address, email or phone number.

Minors under 15: in France, a minor under 15 cannot consent alone to the processing of their data for these services (Article 8 GDPR and Article 45 of the French Data Protection Act); consent must be given jointly by the child and a person with parental responsibility. This is why the cookie banner asks children to have a parent make the choice. Whatever the choice, advertisements remain non-personalised, and audience measurement is never used for advertising.

COPPA (United States): the website is partly directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children within the meaning of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), apart from technical identifiers used solely for the website's internal operations (counting votes, security, statistics, including Google Analytics audience measurement, accepted in the banner and never used for advertising). Ad requests are flagged for child-directed treatment: no behavioural advertising and no remarketing.

If you are a parent and believe that your child has sent us personal information (for example by email or in a request sent with the form), please write to us: it will be deleted.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS only. The games run in an isolated frame, with no access to the Internet. IP address fingerprints are calculated with a secret key and cannot be used to recover the address.

Changes

This policy may change, for example if the website changes. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
